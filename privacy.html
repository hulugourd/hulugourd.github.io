中文

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

Húlu果是一款文玩葫芦收藏与把玩应用。它没有自己的服务器，不需要注册账号，不自动收集个人信息。你的藏品只保存在这部设备上，以及（开启 iCloud 时）你自己的 iCloud 里；开发者读不到你的藏馆。

个人信息处理者与联系方式

  • 个人信息处理者：Zhou Entong（个人开发者）
  • 所在地：新加坡
  • 联系邮箱：hulugourd.app@gmail.com

保存什么，保存在哪里

藏品存档：保存在这部设备的应用沙盒里，包括你的葫芦、盘玩与养护进度、展架与避光柜、叶余额与叶明细、集市的周次与额度，以及用来核对时间的记录。更换或恢复存档时，旧档的副本同样只留在本机。

iCloud 同步：设备登录 iCloud 并允许 Húlu果使用 iCloud 时，压缩后的藏馆存档会自动同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。云端保存最新一份，以及最近 7 个使用日的每日安全副本：只在最新一份读不出时由应用自动取用并提示一次，应用内不列出、也不提供往回恢复。开发者读不到你的私有云档。

重装记录：应用在你的 iCloud 键值存储和本机钥匙串里存一小份重装记录（存档编号、本周货源与买入次数、已赠叶的标记），用来在重装后保留本周货源、不重复赠叶。它不含藏品、余额或叶明细。

购买记录：你通过 App Store 买叶时，应用只记录 Apple 返回的交易号，用来防止重复到账和处理退款；不记录支付方式、姓名或地址。付款由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

偏好设置：语言、墙色、画质等选择，以及本机的同步进度，保存在这部设备上。为了避免切换 Apple 账户后把藏馆写进另一个账户，本机只保存 iCloud 账户记录标识与容器名的散列值；应用不读取你的姓名或邮箱，也不把这个标识或散列值发给开发者。

缓存：皮质贴图、平面图等渲染缓存只在本机，系统可随时清理，不含个人信息。

你主动发送的反馈：应用会打开系统邮件或分享界面，由你确认后发送，应用自己不上传任何内容。诊断日志默认不附带；你勾选后，附件包括 App 与系统版本、画质与缓存设置、省略了具体数据的近期日志，以及本机内存记录（只有资源计数与前后台切换），不含藏品、账户或设备名称。收件人会看到你发送的内容，以及邮件服务提供的发件信息。反馈只用来回复你和改进应用。

不做什么

  • 没有 Húlu果账号，不要求手机号或邮箱。
  • 不自动收集姓名、联系方式、位置、通讯录、照片或设备标识符。
  • 没有统计分析、自动崩溃上报或使用时长统计。
  • 没有广告，不读取广告标识符，不在应用或网站之间跟踪你。
  • 没有第三方 SDK。应用自己只连接 Apple 的 App Store 购买（StoreKit）和你自己的 iCloud。
  • 没有社交、排行或用户之间的交易，你的藏品不会展示给别人。

系统权限

应用不申请相机、定位、通知或通讯录权限。只有在你选择把书签存到相册时，才申请「添加照片」权限；应用不读取相册里的照片。

数据在哪里处理

  • 应用数据保存在你的设备上；开启 iCloud 时，也保存在你自己的 iCloud 里。中国大陆 Apple 账户的 iCloud 由云上贵州（GCBD）运营；其他地区的 iCloud 由 Apple 运营，可能在中国大陆以外处理。
  • App Store 购买由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策，可能在中国大陆以外处理。
  • 你主动发送的反馈经你选择的邮件服务发往开发者邮箱，邮箱服务器可能在中国大陆以外。发不发、发什么，每次都由你决定。
  • 开发者收不到你的藏馆、购买或 iCloud 数据。

保存期限

  • 本机的藏品存档与设置会一直保存，直到你删除应用。
  • iCloud 里的最新存档保留到你删除它为止；每日安全副本最多保留最近 7 个使用日，更早的会被新的替换。
  • 重装记录在删除应用后仍留在本机钥匙串里，用来在重装时认出本周货源与赠叶；它不能恢复你的藏品。
  • 你发来的反馈，开发者在不再需要时删除；你也可以随时要求删除。

你的权利

  • 查看：藏馆、叶明细和设置都在应用里，随时可以查看。
  • 更正：藏品数据由应用的养护规则生成，没有需要更正的账户资料。
  • 删除本机数据：删除 Húlu果应用，这部设备上的藏品存档、设置与缓存会一起删除。
  • 删除 iCloud 数据：打开 iPhone「设置」›（顶部你的姓名）Apple 账户 ›「iCloud」›「管理储存空间」（部分系统版本为「管理账户储存空间」）› Húlu果，选择删除数据。如果应用仍装在手机上，下次打开时会把本机藏馆重新备份到 iCloud；要彻底删除，请先删除应用，再删除 iCloud 数据。
  • 撤回同意：在 iPhone「设置」›（顶部你的姓名）Apple 账户 ›「iCloud」里关掉 Húlu果使用 iCloud，应用就不再同步；已在 iCloud 里的数据可按上一条删除。反馈每一次都由你决定是否发送。
  • 转移：在新设备上登录同一个 Apple 账户并允许 Húlu果使用 iCloud，藏馆会随 iCloud 转过去。
  • 其他请求：发邮件到 hulugourd.app@gmail.com，开发者会在 15 个工作日内答复。
  • 注销：Húlu果没有账号，无需注销。

未成年人

Húlu果不面向 14 周岁以下的儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你未满 14 周岁，请在父母或其他监护人同意并指导下使用本应用，发送反馈前也请先征得监护人同意。监护人如发现孩子未经同意发来了个人信息，请联系开发者删除。应用内购买可以用 Apple 的家长控制（「购买前询问」与屏幕使用时间）管理。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式，本政策会随应用更新一起修订，并更新顶部的日期。「没有服务器、没有账号、不收集个人信息」是这款应用的底线。

English

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Húlu Gourd is a lifestyle app for collecting and caring for ornamental gourds. It has no server of its own, needs no account and does not automatically collect personal information. Your collection is stored only on this device and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see your collection.

Who handles your information

  • Personal information handler: Zhou Entong (individual developer)
  • Location: Singapore
  • Contact email: hulugourd.app@gmail.com

What is stored, and where

Collection archive: stored in the app's sandbox on this device. It holds your gourds, handling and care progress, shelves and shade cabinet, leaf balance and leaf history, the market week and allowances, and the records used to check the time. When an archive is replaced or recovered, a copy of the previous one stays on this device only.

iCloud sync: when the device is signed in to iCloud and Húlu Gourd is allowed to use it, the compressed collection archive syncs automatically to your own private iCloud database. iCloud keeps the latest copy and a daily safety copy for each of the last 7 days you used the app. The app uses a safety copy on its own, with a one-time notice, only if the latest copy cannot be read; it does not list them or offer to roll back to one. The developer cannot read your private iCloud data.

Reinstall record: the app keeps a small reinstall record in your iCloud key-value storage and in this device's Keychain (archive number, this week's offers and purchases, and markers for leaves already given). It lets a reinstall keep this week's offers without giving the same leaves twice. It holds no gourds, balance or leaf history.

Purchase records: when you buy leaves through the App Store, the app records only the transaction number returned by Apple, to avoid crediting a purchase twice and to handle refunds. It never records a payment method, name or address. Payment is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Preferences: your choices such as language, wall colour and picture quality, and this device's sync progress, are stored on this device. So that a collection is never written into a different Apple account after you switch accounts, the device stores only a hash of the iCloud account record identifier and the container name. The app does not read your name or email, and never sends the identifier or its hash to the developer.

Caches: rendering caches such as skin textures and flat pictures stay on this device. The system may clear them at any time, and they contain no personal information.

Feedback you choose to send: the app opens the system Mail or share sheet, and nothing is sent until you confirm it; the app itself uploads nothing. Diagnostic logs are off by default. If you include them, the attachment holds the app and system versions, quality and cache settings, recent logs with data values left out, and this device's memory record (resource counts and foreground/background changes only). It does not include your collection, account or device name. The recipient sees what you send and the sender details your email service adds. Feedback is used only to reply to you and to improve the app.

What the app does not do

  • There is no Húlu Gourd account, and no phone number or email is required.
  • It does not automatically collect names, contact details, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • There is no analytics, automatic crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • There is no advertising; the app does not read the advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites.
  • There are no third-party SDKs. The app itself connects only to Apple's App Store purchases (StoreKit) and your own iCloud.
  • There is no social feature, ranking or trading between users, and your collection is never shown to anyone else.

System permissions

The app asks for no camera, location, notification or contacts permission. It asks to add to Photos only when you choose to save a bookmark there, and it never reads the photos in your library.

Where data is processed

  • App data is stored on your device and, with iCloud on, in your own iCloud. iCloud for Apple accounts in China mainland is operated by GCBD (Guizhou-Cloud Big Data); iCloud elsewhere is operated by Apple and may be processed outside China mainland.
  • App Store purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and may be processed outside China mainland.
  • Feedback you choose to send travels through your email service to the developer's mailbox, whose servers may be outside China mainland. Whether to send it, and what to include, is your choice each time.
  • The developer receives none of your collection, purchase or iCloud data.

How long data is kept

  • The collection archive and settings stay on this device until you delete the app.
  • The latest archive in iCloud stays until you delete it. Daily safety copies cover at most the last 7 days of use; older ones are replaced by newer ones.
  • The reinstall record stays in this device's Keychain after the app is deleted, so that a reinstall recognises this week's offers and leaf gifts. It cannot restore your gourds.
  • Feedback you send is deleted by the developer once it is no longer needed, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

  • View: your collection, leaf history and settings are all in the app, and you can look at them at any time.
  • Correct: collection data is produced by the app's care rules; there is no account information to correct.
  • Delete data on this device: delete the Húlu Gourd app, and the collection archive, settings and caches on this device are deleted with it.
  • Delete iCloud data: open iPhone Settings › your name (Apple Account) › iCloud › Manage Storage (Manage Account Storage on some versions) › Húlu Gourd, and choose to delete its data. If the app is still installed, it backs up the collection on this device to iCloud again the next time it opens; to remove it completely, delete the app first, then its iCloud data.
  • Withdraw consent: in iPhone Settings › your name (Apple Account) › iCloud, turn off iCloud for Húlu Gourd, and the app stops syncing. Data already in iCloud can be deleted as described above. Feedback is only ever sent when you choose to send it.
  • Move your data: sign in to the same Apple Account on a new device and allow Húlu Gourd to use iCloud, and your collection follows through iCloud.
  • Other requests: email hulugourd.app@gmail.com; the developer will reply within 15 working days.
  • Close an account: Húlu Gourd has no account, so there is nothing to close.

Children

Húlu Gourd is not designed for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you are under 14, please use the app with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian, and ask them before sending feedback. A parent or guardian who finds that a child has sent personal information without consent can contact the developer to have it deleted. In-app purchases can be managed with Apple's parental controls (Ask to Buy and Screen Time).

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be revised together with that update and the date at the top will change. "No server, no account, no collection of personal information" is the baseline of this app.

This policy is available in Chinese and English. If the two differ, the Chinese text prevails.
