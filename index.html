联系与支持

有问题或建议，请发邮件到 hulugourd.app@gmail.com，开发者会在 15 个工作日内答复。也可以在应用里点「设置 › 联系开发者」写信。

换手机或重装后，藏品还在吗？

在新设备上登录同一个 Apple 账户，并在 iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › iCloud」里允许 Húlu果使用 iCloud，打开应用后藏馆会自动从 iCloud 取回。没有开启 iCloud 时，藏品只在原来那部手机上。

买的叶没有到账？

点主页右上角的叶余额，打开「添些叶」，先点「检查未到账的购买」；换了手机或重装后，点「恢复购买」补回这部手机上还没记过的购买。

怎样申请退款？

App Store 的购买由 Apple 处理，请在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

怎样删除我的数据？

删除应用即删除本机数据；iCloud 里的数据按隐私政策「你的权利」一节删除。应用没有账号，开发者也收不到你的藏馆。

A quiet app for collecting and caring for ornamental gourds. Privacy Policy

Contact and support

For questions or suggestions, email hulugourd.app@gmail.com. The developer replies within 15 working days. You can also write from the app: Settings › Contact the Developer.

Will my collection survive a new phone or a reinstall?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new device and allow Húlu Gourd to use iCloud (iPhone Settings › Apple Account › iCloud). When you open the app, your collection comes back from iCloud. Without iCloud, the collection stays only on the original phone.

I bought leaves but they haven't arrived

Tap the leaf balance at the top right of the home screen to open "A few more leaves", then tap "Check uncredited purchases". After a reinstall or on a new phone, tap "Restore Purchases" to add any purchases this phone hasn't recorded yet.

How do I ask for a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes the data on your phone. To delete the iCloud data, follow "Your rights" in the privacy policy. There is no account, and the developer never receives your collection.
